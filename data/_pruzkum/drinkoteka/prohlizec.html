Bar řemeslného piva v Praze s výhledem na Vltavu

Drinkotéka je craft beer bar v Praze na Náplavce s pravidelně obměňovanou nabídkou čepovaných piv a limitovanými edicemi z českých a evropských pivovarů.

Široký výběr moderních stylů craftového piva

Čepujeme čerstvé IPA, NEIPA, kyselá piva i stouty z českých a zahraničních craft pivovarů. Nabídka se průběžně mění, takže je vždy co nového ochutnat.

Pivotéka a E-shop

Nabízíme raritní kousky a limitované edice z vybraných pivovarů. Piva jsou určená s sebou nebo k vychutnání přímo u nás na zahrádce. E-shop nabízí rychlé doručení po Praze nebo následující den po celé republice.

Where is Drinkøtéka in Prague, and how do I get there?

Drinkøtéka is at Rašínovo nábřeží 384/56, Prague 2 (Nové Město), right on the Vltava riverside at Náplavka. The nearest tram stop is Palackého náměstí. Both local beer fans and visitors discovering Czech craft beer are welcome.

What are Drinkøtéka's opening hours?

Monday to Friday 15:00 to 22:00, Saturday 12:00 to 22:00, closed on Sunday.

Where can I find the best craft beer in Prague?

Drinkøtéka is a good place to start. We run 10 taps that rotate almost daily and over 200 cans and bottles, and we choose beer by quality: we tap only highly rated releases, and you can see each beer's Untappd rating on our live menu. The range covers independent Czech breweries and selected international ones, with styles from Czech lagers and NEIPAs to West Coast IPAs, DIPAs, sours, stouts and non-alcoholic craft beer. We keep fewer taps on purpose and pour only what we rate highly.

Does Drinkøtéka have a bottle shop and an online shop?

Yes. The bottle shop is a curated selection of over 200 cans and bottles, including rare and limited releases from Czech and international breweries. You can also browse and order at shop.drinkoteka.cz, with delivery across the Czech Republic and Europe.

Can I drink my beer outside?

Yes. On warm days you will often see people standing outside Drinkøtéka with a beer. In summer there is outdoor seating in front of the bar, or you can take your beer across the road to the riverside.

Where can I drink beer with a view of the Vltava and Prague Castle?

Drinkøtéka sits right on the Vltava riverside at Náplavka. Take your beer to the outdoor seating in front of the bar, or cross the road to the riverside, where you get a view of the river and Prague Castle. It is a popular spot around sunset.

Can I visit Drinkøtéka during the Náplavka Saturday farmers market?

Yes. The Saturday farmers market runs along Rašínovo nábřeží, and Drinkøtéka opens at 12:00. Stop in for a freshly poured Czech craft beer, or pick up a can or bottle to take with you along the riverside. Check the market's current schedule before visiting.

How much does a beer cost, and do I need a reservation?

Prices depend on the beer, style and size, so check the current tap list or ask our team for today's prices. We accept cash in CZK and cards, including contactless. No reservation is needed, just walk in.

Can your staff help me choose a beer?

Yes. Our bartenders are keen beer enthusiasts, and many of them were Drinkøtéka regulars before joining the team, so they are good at reading what you want. Tell them the styles you like or want to avoid, your budget, or that it is a gift, and they will point you to something. We can help in English, Czech, Ukrainian and German.

Does Drinkøtéka host tap takeovers and meet-the-brewer events?

Yes. We regularly host tap takeovers and meet-the-brewer events, where a guest Czech or international brewery takes over the taps and you can taste their beers fresh and meet the brewers. Follow us on Instagram or Untappd for upcoming dates.

Can I buy a craft beer gift in Prague, or send one from abroad?

Yes. Instead of a fixed box, our bartenders put together a personalised craft beer gift to the recipient's taste, preferred styles and your budget, with gift wrapping. You can pick it up in the bottle shop, order through our online shop, or arrange a gift for someone in Prague while you are abroad. For delivery within Prague we can add a greeting card with a handwritten message and deliver on a specific date arranged in advance, and Prague delivery is 179 CZK. Message us on WhatsApp with the recipient, occasion and any styles to include or avoid.
